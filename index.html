FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need To Know

Got questions about how status payouts, M-Pesa verification, and packages work? We've got answers.

How do I receive my earnings after posting? ▼

Once you download and post an active product banner to your WhatsApp Status, leave it active for 18 hours. Take a clear screenshot of your viewer count and upload it in your Dashboard. Our automated system calculates your earnings (Views × Tier Rate) and credits your balance instantly for M-Pesa withdrawal.

Why is there an 18-hour cooldown between submissions? ▼

Corporate advertisers pay for genuine impressions that remain visible on your status for a substantial portion of the 24-hour cycle. The 18-hour cooldown ensures maximum brand exposure and protects the high payout rates our partners enjoy.

How do I pay and activate my package? ▼

You can activate via Safaricom M-Pesa Lipa na M-Pesa using Paybill 522533 and Account 8106675. After sending the exact fee (KSh 250 for Starter Z, KSh 500 for Exclusive, or KSh 1,200 for Premium), paste your M-Pesa confirmation code in your dashboard to activate immediately.

Can I upgrade from Starter Z to Exclusive or Premium later? ▼

Yes, absolutely! You can upgrade your tier at any time directly from the Packages page. Your earnings per view will immediately reflect the higher payout multiplier on your next task.

How do businesses book ads on Almai Ads? ▼

Business owners and agencies can click "Book a Business Campaign" or tap the green "Chat with Us" WhatsApp button at the bottom right to speak directly with our advertising team and customize a targeted Kenyan campaign.
